VISION, VALOR & INTEGRITY

Founding Principles

The Junior Prefects' Guild of Piliyandala Central College serves as the primary stepping stone for developing student leadership. Established to bridge administrative oversight and the student body, the guild trains promising individuals to exhibit strict accountability, command respect through empathy, and uphold school policies with dignity.

Core Selection Criteria

Guild members are hand-picked based on exceptional parameters. Candidates must display superior academic persistence, active participation in extra-curricular activities or sports, and an untarnished record of personal behavioral conduct. Final validation from sectional heads and class teachers is required.

GOVERNANCE & ADVISORY GUIDANCE

The operational directives, training camps, and executive decisions of the Junior Prefects' Guild are continuously monitored and guided by an elite administrative panel:

  • Patron: The Principal of Piliyandala Central College
  • Advisory Body: Disciplinary Committee & Sectional Heads
  • Mentorship Core: Teachers-in-Charge of the Prefects' Guild
