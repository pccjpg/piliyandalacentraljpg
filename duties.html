THE PREFECTURAL CODE OF CONDUCT

A junior prefect must maintain personal discipline that stands as an example for the rest of the student body. Our strict internal code centers around three absolute tenets:

  • Absolute Impartiality: Standard regulations must be enforced evenly across all students, without showing personal favoritism or bias toward friends or peers.
  • Punctuality & Reliability: Arriving at least 15 minutes before assigned shift times and securing proper coverage from deputy heads if an emergency absence arises.
  • Dignified Presentation: Displaying an unblemished uniform, clean badge presentation, and using polite but firm language during all public interactions.
